\QBlock{Q1}{An object starts with an initial velocity of $6.2 \, \text{m/s}$ and moves along a straight line with a constant acceleration of $-2.3 \, \text{m/s}^2$. What is the displacement of the object when its velocity is $-7.5 \, \text{m/s}$?}{
  \Wrong{-20.1 m}{This answer results from using incorrect signs for the initial and final velocities.}
  \Wrong{-25.4 m}{This answer occurs from ignoring the negative sign in acceleration.}
  \Correct{-27.0 m}{To find the displacement, use the equation $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$, where $v = -7.5 \, \text{m/s}$, $u = 6.2 \, \text{m/s}$, and $a = -2.3 \, \text{m/s}^2$. Solving for $s$, $s = \frac{v^2 - u^2}{2a}$ yields $s = \frac{(-7.5)^2 - (6.2)^2}{2 \times (-2.3)} = -27.0 \, \text{m}$. This result is the correct displacement.}
  \Wrong{-30.7 m}{This option assumes acceleration was positive, affecting the calculation of displacement.}
}
